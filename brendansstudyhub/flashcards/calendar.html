Connect Google Calendar
Paste your Google Calendar embed URL to view your schedule right here in Study Hub.
1. Go to calendar.google.com
2. Click ⚙️ Settings → select your calendar on the left
3. Scroll down to "Integrate calendar"
4. Copy the Embed code URL (the src="..." part)
5. Paste it below — it starts with https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?...
